Service 01

Cabinetry that fits like it was always there.

Enquire about this service

Whether it’s a full set of kitchen cabinets or a single vanity, we build to the space you have rather than forcing a standard size to fit. Clean, modern profiles and solid wood, especially white oak, are where we do our best work.

  • Kitchen cabinets for smaller kitchens and focused remodels
  • Bathroom vanities, including resizing pieces that don’t fit
  • Custom-fit cabinetry for awkward or non-standard spaces
  • Modifications to existing cabinetry

Ask us
Can you build a whole kitchen?

We build kitchen cabinetry for smaller kitchens and focused remodels. Tell us about your space and we’ll talk through what’s possible.

Can you resize a vanity I already have?

Yes. Resizing an oversized vanity was one of the shop’s first jobs, and modifications are still part of what we do.

Do you work with laminate?

Our focus is solid wood, especially white oak. We don’t specialise in laminate or Formica work.

Ask us
Where are you based?

Our woodshop is in Campbell, right beside the Parfett Construction office. Most of our cabinetry work is for homes in and around Campbell.

Can you modify cabinets I already have?

Yes. Alongside new cabinetry, we can modify existing cabinets and resize pieces that don’t quite fit, like a vanity that’s too large for the room.

What wood do you work with?

Solid wood is our focus, and white oak is a favourite for clean, modern interiors. We don’t specialise in laminate or Formica work.

Can you build a hidden bookshelf door?

Yes. Concealed bookshelf doors are one of our specialities: a working bookshelf that swings open to a room or closet behind it.

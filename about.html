Our story

Built on problem-solving.

Rob Parfett started woodworking in his garage, making the custom pieces his construction clients couldn’t buy anywhere else.

One client needed an oversized vanity cut down to fit. Another needed a teak duck board for a shower. Over time those one-off fixes turned into a full woodshop, now in its own space beside the Parfett Construction office in Campbell.

Rob still loves the detailed finish carpentry and the unusual dimensional and design problems that make each project different.

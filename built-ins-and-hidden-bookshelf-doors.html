Ask us
What is a hidden bookshelf door?

It’s a fully working bookshelf that also acts as a door, swinging open to reveal a room, closet or storage space behind it.

Do you make standard interior doors?

Conventional door manufacturing isn’t part of our offer. Hidden bookshelf doors are a specific woodworking speciality.

Can built-ins match my existing cabinetry?

We design built-ins to suit the room, including wood species and finish. Share photos and we’ll talk through options.
